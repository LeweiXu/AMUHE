# Chapter 141: Nyan-nyan

The unveiling ceremony itself held no particular substance.

Words of congratulation from each nation were read aloud for the coronation of the Queen of Alberan, appropriate gifts were received.

Flowery phrases――a pointless exchange of formality and surface pleasantries.

What mattered was the meeting that followed.

In a conference room of the royal castle, seated around a rectangular table were Queen Kreschenta, Grand Priest Zarnalibea, Vice Chairman Olgan of the Galshan Republic, Chancellor Krolus of the Elderant Kingdom――and Archbishop Elbebart of the Empire's western diocese.

From the other nations came their number two or number three.

Those with the authority to decide the course of future events by their own will.

Elbebart, who held no such authority and was several ranks below the rest, wiped cold sweat from his brow as his face paled.

He had drawn the short straw on account of his nation's leadership saving face.

This was a forum for discussion between nations.

Properly, one of Elbebart's modest stature――a Grand Priest overseeing a mere western diocese――had no business being here; it should have been the Grand Priest Primate, or at the very least a Cardinal, sitting in that chair.

He clearly did not belong in a place like this.

*Won't this end already*, he thought, looking visibly uncomfortable and glancing to his side.

Seated there was Prince Klauze of the minor southwestern Kingdom of Kuslan――vastly inferior to the four great nations.

Yet as befitted royalty, he sat with a dignified bearing even in this situation.

Elbebart had no allies.

"However, Your Majesty. You speak of peace, but if that is your position, should you not first settle accounts for the kingdom's past aggression?"

The one who spoke was Vice Chairman Olgan of the Galshan Republic.

Kreschenta turned her gaze to him.

A face much like the fearsome southern general Dagren Garkha――bearded and rough-hewn.

He looked more suited to be a bandit chief.

Yet to hear that he had been born royalty was nothing short of astonishing.

The Galshan Republic had only been a republic for about twenty years.

Before that, it had been a monarchical state just like Alberan, but this man, despite being royalty himself, had launched a rebellion to correct the corruption of the royal house.

After toppling the monarchy and establishing the republic, he had declared that taking the chairman's seat himself "would be no different from usurpation" and taken the vice chairman's seat instead――to Kreschenta, he was a baffling, unhinged individual.

She disliked him, primarily because his voice was too loud.

"In the civil war twenty years ago――the northern territory the kingdom seized. The lament of the people driven from their land has yet to fade. What do you say to that?"

Kreschenta tilted her head as if troubled.

"That war was undertaken at the request of the Galshan royal house of the time. It was by no means an invasion……and besides, the discussions on that matter concluded fifteen years ago, with your nation's consent."

"Consent. I see, you call that a proper discussion. A meeting where military force was dangled before our weakened, depleted nation?"

Olgan asked as if issuing a challenge.

These were events from before Kreschenta was born. She could not possibly know the details.

"Of course, that was two rulers ago. I bear no ill will toward you, the current Queen. But as the one who inherits the Kingdom of Alberan, I believe that before speaking of peace, you should first settle accounts for what Alberan has done. ……If Your Majesty truly desires peace."

――*Otherwise, the people driven from their homes cannot be satisfied.*

Olgan's words were an unmistakable threat.

Kreschenta received them with a smile.

"I see. Then would paying money suffice?"

Kreschenta spoke, and before the frowning Olgan could form a reply, she continued.

"That is not what you mean, is it. The hatred and grudges born of war are not the sort of thing that vanishes the next day."

Show weakness once, and it was over.

In diplomacy, you would only be devoured.

She must maintain the upper hand through the atmosphere and her words at all times.

"Galshan and Alberan have fought each other many times over the long years……and all the resentment born from that killing cannot be resolved by a single payment of coin, can it?"

Kreschenta continued.

"Go back a few more decades, and that land belonged to the Kingdom of Alberan. The grandparents of the people Olgan-sama speaks of――those whose homeland was ravaged and seized by Galshan. We have been repeating this cycle. Do you not find it fruitless?"

"Do you intend to go back a thousand years next?"

"Fufu, that is not what I mean."

Kreschenta laughed, looking amused.

"Your concern for your people is admirable, Olgan-sama. But resolving it will require a long stretch of time, I think."

Olgan folded his arms, wondering what she was getting at, and stared into Kreschenta's eyes.

"……If there are people who wish to return to their homeland, and they are truly of that land, the kingdom will accept them to the fullest extent. The south is fertile land, with many enterprises to be undertaken, and we could arrange housing and employment. What has been lost cannot be reclaimed, but new foundations can be built."

She met his gaze squarely, her smile growing broader.

What she wore was a somewhat impudent grin.

"All that is needed beyond that is for our nations to join hands, and the passage of time for the hatred to fade and for people to settle into their new lives. ……How does that sound? If the welfare of your people is truly your first concern, this should not be a bad proposal for either nation. Though of course, I must add the caveat――if Olgan-sama truly desires peace."

The young queen turned his own words back at him with apparent delight.

Then she brought to her lips the cloyingly sweet tea her red-haired servant Bery had brewed behind her.

The half-provocative words made the palace nobles in attendance stiffen, but what echoed through the room was a booming laugh.

"Hahaha! Well, well! Forgive my rude question. For one so young, you did not flinch, and you carry yourself with gravitas. And quite the sharp mind, at that. My apologies."

"Please think nothing of it, Olgan-sama. I am happy to hear you say so."

Swaying the red-gold of her hair.

The fairy-like queen wore a gentle smile upon her beauty.

Internally, she was resisting the urge to cover her ears against his booming voice.

His question had likely been a test of Kreschenta.

If she were a mere idealist――if she cowered before the prospect of war and offered up money as told, he had planned to bleed her dry.

War was expensive; the other approach was far more profitable.

Drain the kingdom's strength that way, then invade and carve up the territory.

"I was astonished to hear that you had unseated the Black Lion――the Grand Duke of Alberan――and claimed the throne, but indeed, now I understand. The kingdom's future looks bright."

The proof was in how Olgan buried her proposal under further pleasantries.

He had no intention of agreeing to anything that did not place Galshan in the superior position――and an invasion of Alberan was likely all but a settled matter.

Kreschenta paid it no particular mind and went along.

"Thank you. If peace can be brought to the kingdom henceforth, I should like that as well."

"Those are words I cannot let pass."

The one who cut in from the side was Chancellor Krolus of the Elderant Kingdom.

The old man toyed with his long beard and glared at Kreschenta.

"It sounds as though you are saying the threat to peace lies elsewhere. History makes plain which nation has always visited calamity upon its neighbors."

"You mean Alberan?"

"Indeed. When Alberan speaks of peace, it is merely a preparatory period for the next invasion. How many times has our nation been burned because it was fooled by those words?"

*Quite right, I could not agree more*, Kreschenta thought, while keeping her smile intact.

The Elderant Kingdom called itself a kingdom, but unlike the absolute monarchy of Alberan, it was closer to a tribal confederation of barbarians.

More a union than a feudal state, really, where the chief of the strongest tribe became leader and assumed the title of King of Elderant.

Once a king was decided, the tribal chiefs swore their loyalty, but naturally, relations between tribes were poor, and internal conflicts were frequent.

Furthermore, each time a king died, the powerful tribes warred over the succession, wearing each other down――at which point Alberan would manufacture some pretext and carve off territory. This was routine.

"With all due respect, not a day passes without reports of provocative border crossings and raids by Elderant forces in the kingdom's west. Hearing such reports, we must respond――I would ask your nation as well to cooperate in not sowing the seeds of war."

"Mere bandits raiding, and you lay the blame on our nation."

Naturally, he would never admit to such things.

Elderant held territory stretching to the western mountains, as vast as the kingdom's, but the reason it lagged behind the other neighboring nations was surely due to such internal squabbling.

Technologically and culturally it was reasonably civilized, but in mentality, they were barbarians. A nation-shaped bandit consortium, and an exceedingly vexing existence.

That said, the kingdom had already taken most of the fertile plains along the western border; what remained was forest.

Since the bulk of Elderant's territory was forest, it would be difficult to conquer outright, and the returns would be meager.

The reason it still survived as a nation was precisely that, and Kreschenta rather wished the previous kings had simply wiped it out before her time.

The current king hailed from Elderant's eastern regions and was naturally not well-disposed toward Alberan.

Judging from the chancellor's aggressive posture, an attack was all but certain.

South and west alike, the calculus was the same――there was no reason not to attack the kingdom.

Even the Elsren to the east had sent an utterly meaningless envoy, and there seemed to be no shortage of incompetents over there either.

Any chance of turning the situation around from here seemed slim, and Kreschenta quickly gave up on averting war, wishing only for this meeting to end as quickly as possible.

* * *

"That queen is quite the formidable one."

"Taken a liking to her?"

"Oh yes, I have. We start preparing the military the moment we get back."

"You liked her, so you're going to attack?……And you want to start preparations now?"

In a corridor of white forged-stone――Vice Chairman Olgan's words drew a look of exasperation from the tall, slender Master-at-Arms Zalvarg.

An unremarkable face, nothing noteworthy about it――despite holding the position of Galshan's Master-at-Arms, the highest-ranking military officer, Zalvarg's features lacked flair.

Originally neither noble nor anything else, he had been a commoner, a cobbler. Twenty years ago, during the civil war, he had joined the rebel side, distinguished himself on the battlefield, and risen to become Olgan's chief aide, the most trusted warrior at his side.

He accompanied Olgan on this visit as head of the guard.

Olgan himself cared nothing for power, but Galshan's government was sustained by Olgan's personal standing.

Regardless of his title as vice chairman, he was in substance the leader of Galshan, a man who must never be lost.

The level of security afforded by having Master-at-Arms Zalvarg serve as his guard was only natural.

"If we attack, we'll be facing that Garkha. Even years of preparation might not be enough."

Dagren Garkha.

The fierce general who had defeated Galshan time and again.

One could fight magical beast elephants with such composure only if one were that man.

An iron-wall formation that yet never lost its flexibility――for Galshan, when they imagined the kingdom's army, the first image that came to mind was that man.

The reason Olgan, who had cut short the campaign against northwestern Elderant and was watching for an opportunity, had been unable to act during this civil war was that Garkha had been watching the south without a moment's lapse.

Engaging him while forces were committed to Elderant was too dangerous.

The reason he had accepted Kreschenta's invitation was partly a desire to see her for himself, but above all, to give Galshan time to prepare.

A few months or a year would not be enough for a nation to fully recover from a civil war.

Unlike ordinary warfare, a civil war inflicted the costs of both friend and foe on a single nation's treasury.

Given the same span of time, Galshan, which had surplus capacity to begin with, would naturally gain the upper hand.

A proposal from the Empire had come before their departure from Galshan.

Unlike last time, this would likely be a joint operation.

The same request had been made during the Empire's previous invasion, but Olgan had refused because ambushing an enemy from behind felt dishonorable.

Olgan knew no thrill greater than staking one's life and pride on battle.

He had chosen Garkha as his worthy adversary, and had no desire to stoop to the cowardice of striking him from behind.

"……Hm?"

What caught Olgan's eye was the courtyard.

There, a single large man and a beautiful girl in a pale sky-blue dress with silver hair.

"Hmm, this is……"

"Hahaha, fine craftsmanship, isn't it? This was made long ago, when Corps Commander Melkikos scolded me for not caring about my armor, and I had the same artisan who made Bogan-sama's……"

At the ceremony, Kokys had attended in his armor, and while waiting for Kreschenta――or rather, for Bery, who was attending Kreschenta――Krische had happened upon him.

Waiting, she had been chatting with him a little.

Krische had taken an interest in his imposing, tiger-shaped helm.

Kokys removed the helm to show her, explaining its history.

"As you can see, a tiger――"

"Nyan-nyan."

Krische looked up, eyes sparkling.

"Ehehe, it's a cat, right? Bery loves them. They go meow-meow. Do you like them too, Kokys?"

"……Eh? Ah, no, it's not a cat, you see……"

"……It's true that using just Kokys instead of Corps Commander Agrand is fine, I suppose……but actually, among the soldiers, there's a trend of giving people affectionate nicknames."

"A-affectionate nicknames?"

"Yes, you know, like Skeleton. Ehehe, Krische was the one who came up with Skeleton, you know. Because he has a skeleton-like face!"

"S-so I've heard……"

That terrifying nickname that no one dared speak aloud――the only one who actually called Elrouga that was Krische, and the "trend" of nicknames existed not in the military but only within Krische's head.

Kokys took the most diplomatic approach he could.

"Since you'll be working under Krische now, Krische thought it would be nice to give Kokys a nickname instead of just calling you by your name. Krische had a hard time thinking of one, but just now it came to Krische in a flash. From now on, Krische will call you Nyan-nyan."

"……What?"

"Woof-woof is Varkus Corps Commander's nickname, and look, you two even have a similar feel. The pair of you as Woof-woof and Nyan-nyan is just wonderful! It's so easy to say."

She bounced lightly on her toes. Krische was in exceptionally high spirits.

The beaming smile she had never once directed at him since they met――it held a power that would make even the famously fierce Kokys flinch.

"Here, Nyan-nyan, your helmet back."

She stretched on tiptoe, gave a small hop, and placed the "imposing, tiger-shaped helm" on Kokys's head, then nodded in satisfaction several times.

Kokys turned his gaze to the nearby pond and stared at the reflection of the armored giant.

A body sheathed in muscle and steel armor, the body of a warrior.

And that was Nyan-nyan.

It was madness.

――*So this is how Corps Commander Faren and Granmeld fell.*

*I will not make the same mistake.*

Kokys hurriedly spoke up.

"W-wait, please, Krische-sama, th-that nickname is a bit much……"

"……Oh? Someone's coming. Is that an acquaintance of yours, Nyan-nyan?"

But Krische's attention was already elsewhere.

The sweet voice calling him "Nyan-nyan"――the sheer naturalness of it told Kokys, as if confirming what he already feared, that there was no going back.

"Mm……that's someone from the ceremony earlier. Vice Chairman Olgan of the Galshan Republic?"

Though lacking the sheer mass of Kokys, the man approaching was still unmistakably large.

Muscles threatening to burst his formal attire, a bearded middle-aged man strode toward them.

"Well now, a familiar face. From Christand……"

"Corps Commander Agrand, Your Excellency."

Kokys straightened instinctively, clicking his heels together.

When facing a foreign dignitary, one clasped one's hands behind one's back to indicate lack of hostile intent.

"Right, the Christand corps commander. When it's been so many years, even my memory gets hazy. Though a man like you is not one I'd easily forget."

Krische observed Kokys's demeanor, then lightly lifted the hem of her skirt and curtsied.

She looked every inch a princess, carrying herself with effortless grace.

Utterly unrecognizable as the fiend who had, moments prior, been forcing a subordinate to accept the name "Nyan-nyan."

"……And this one was at Her Majesty's side."

"This is Krische Alberinea Christand-sama."

Kokys answered without missing a beat.

Krische would not have minded, but it was customary for royalty not to introduce themselves.

Having someone at their side make the introduction was standard, and Kokys treated Krische as royalty for the occasion.

He was also somewhat anxious about letting Krische talk freely with a foreign dignitary.

"……Alberinea."

Who it was that stood beside Queen Kreschenta――on that question, everyone had been left wondering.

The queen had an elder sister, and that sister, it was rumored, was the hero who had defeated the generals that sided with Gildanstein and struck down Gildanstein himself during the civil war.

Everyone suspected it was her, but no one could openly ask.

Asking a royal their name was, in any nation, the height of discourtesy.

One was expected to research the matter in advance or, as now, wait for an introduction from the other party.

It was customary for the host side to ensure no embarrassment.

Rinea was the honorary title denoting a knight of the kingdom, and alongside it was the unfamiliar title Alberinea, evidently derived from the kingdom's name.

This girl was surely the one.

Her face was doll-like and expressionless, with only her observing violet eyes gleaming inorganically.

No warmth, no cheerful smile.

Resembling Kreschenta yet not――a beauty that sent a chill down the spine. The mana she wore was what could only be called serene.

Physical enhancement――virtual musculature built from mana.

Noticing it was already deployed, Olgan furrowed his brow.

He considered whether she was wary of him, and instinctively felt that was not the case.

Her stance had none of the tension, stiffness, or distortion of a readied body.

From the beginning, as a matter of daily habit.

She simply lived this way, always.

The concentration and stability were fearsome.

Using mana as a matter of daily life was extraordinarily difficult.

Compared to one's physical muscles, mana was overwhelmingly powerful――manageable on a battlefield, but fine control in everyday situations was another matter entirely.

When Olgan had heard the rumors of Alberan's other princess, he had been half-skeptical.

The sword princess who, despite losing the kingdom's hero Bogan Christand, had slain the ever-victorious Aulgorn Hilkintos and cut down Gildanstein, the Black Lion of Alberan.

Word was she was a girl not yet fifteen.

The battlefield was not so forgiving as to let such a girl dance upon it.

Having known the battlefield more intimately than anyone, Olgan had doubted.

He had assumed it was merely someone else's glory, claimed for her.

Yet, seeing the girl before him, he could not dismiss it as impossible.

So greatly did this girl's presence unsettle something deep in Olgan.

She was unlike any warrior he had ever seen.

Not the oppressive presence of someone like Kokys.

A crawling sensation along the spine――the same thing he had felt long ago, as a child, when he stood close to a captured magical beast.

An instinctual fear.

"……I see. Alberinea――up close, it is clear. You seem every bit the woman the rumors describe."

Olgan extended his right hand.

Krische peered at it curiously, then offered her own with a faintly reluctant look.

His hand was as hairy as his face. It looked unsanitary.

"As a fellow warrior, it is an honor to meet you. Perhaps, someday not too distant, we shall meet again."

"Ah……thank you."

As her right hand was grasped, Krische considered how she would kill him if he attacked, and gave a slight bow.

She did not like the handshake itself, as it fundamentally meant giving up the freedom of one hand, and on top of that, his hand was hairy and slightly sweaty. All the more unsanitary.

Already, in Krische's mind, Olgan's rating had tipped firmly toward "dislike."

The moment her hand was released, Krische promptly took out a handkerchief and carefully wiped her hand.

Olgan paid it no mind, but the faces of Kokys and Master-at-Arms Zalvarg went rigid.

It was spectacularly rude, breathtakingly discourteous.

Depending on the circumstances, it could escalate into an international incident.

That said, Krische was a self-proclaimed well-mannered young lady――in her mind, cleaning one's hands after touching something dirty was a perfectly rational custom, and a hygienic, sensible gesture at that.

Any breach of etiquette she was unaware of simply did not register.

Without a single qualm, she meticulously cleaned the hand that had been shaken, corner to corner.

Kokys and Zalvarg watched her, dumbfounded.

"There are many beasts in Galshan that cannot be seen in the kingdom. When next we meet, I should very much like to show them to Alberinea."

"Um……thank you……?"

It was a declaration of war of sorts, but Krische simply tilted her head, thinking it was perhaps some kind of customary greeting.

He was offering to show her various beasts. She ought to express her gratitude, at least.

That was the extent of her understanding.

The roundabout suggestion of meeting on the battlefield sailed completely over her head.

"Until then. Come, Zalvarg."

But unaware that his words had not landed at all, Olgan swept his cloak in a dramatic flourish, his declaration complete, and strode away.

Zalvarg fell in step behind him.

"Did you see that, Zalvarg? That one is a monster――not to be underestimated by appearances."

"……She certainly is something. Especially the part where she immediately wiped her hand with a handkerchief after a handshake."

"Hm?"

"No, never mind. ……Did you take a liking to that one as well?"

"I did. Heh, how entertaining, this kingdom. ……The fire burns hotter now."

Olgan laughed like a beast, and wore a beast's grin.

* * *

"What's wrong? Nyan-nyan."

"I-it's not just the nickname――that was not good, Krische-sama. Wiping your hand right after a handshake is――"

"……? Krische likes to keep her hands clean."

"Th-that's not the point……"

Convincing Krische that wiping one's hand after a handshake was rude required about half an hour after that.

# Chapter 142: The Palace Incident

For example, heavy infantry alone could not kill skirmishers.

Unable to catch the lightly equipped troops, they would simply be harassed to death.

Yet skirmishers alone could not resist cavalry.

Borne down upon by speed, pulled into close-quarters range, they had no armor to protect them.

But that cavalry could not kill infantry that had set their spears.

Even if the cavalry attempted a charge using speed and mass, those spear points would impale horse and rider both.

Each stood in a three-way deadlock, and that was why an army fielded all three.

Cavalry against the enemy's skirmishers. Spears against the enemy's cavalry. Arrows against the enemy's spearmen.

But the Black Flag Special Service was both cavalry and spear. Already possessing two of the three at an extremely high level, if long-range firepower were added on top, there would be nothing left that could oppose them.

Outside the walls of Albernaria.

In the large-scale training ground built there, the Black Flag Special Service was drilling.

The Black Flag's training grounds were generally tucked away in a corner, beside the city walls.

A patch surrounded by trees not quite dense enough to be called a forest was allocated for their exercises.

Within the grove, javelins thrown by the Black Flag soldiers punched through trees, their impacts thundering――and the snap of powerful bowstrings echoed from all around.

"Vulture, how goes the progress of the drills?"

"Without issue. Very smooth indeed. Krische-sama, Gallen-sama."

Gallen, accompanying Krische, surveyed the scene with evident interest.

They had been inspecting the other corps together, and this was the last stop.

It was his first time observing the training of the Black Flag Special Service, which was essentially Krische's direct command, and everything felt fresh.

This was supposed to be a new-recruit training session, yet there was no sign of the standard drills――column marching, formation defense, and the like.

"Perhaps because the unit's name became known through its performance in the civil war. The recruits sent by the selection unit are all brimming with enthusiasm, proud to have been chosen for the kingdom's finest. Even when ordered through grueling drills, they grit their teeth and endure. Ideal material for new recruits."

"……I see, that's good to hear. Those look like javelin exercises, but the equipment seems a bit different."

The sound that had been echoing for some time was the thud of trees being gouged.

What the Black Flag soldiers gripped and hurled at the trees were dully gleaming iron rods――with hemp cloth wound around the grip, the entire shaft appeared to be solid iron.

"Just iron bars. Regular javelins break too often, and the replacement costs added up, so Krische-sama told me to come up with something. Mia."

"Ah, yes."

Mia grabbed one of the iron bars leaning against a rack and brought it over, presenting it to Gallen.

Gallen nearly staggered under its weight, but caught himself.

For Gallen, who did not wield mana, it was far too heavy.

"……They're throwing this? Mana users truly are built differently. An old man like me couldn't hope to throw this."

Gallen returned the iron javelin to Mia with an exasperated look.

The girl, far smaller and more slender than Gallen, received it effortlessly and bowed her head.

It was likely down to innate mana capacity. Setting aside swordsmanship, Mia was among the strongest in the unit in terms of raw physical power.

"Haha, even among us, only a handful can throw these properly. More than javelin training, what they're doing here is practice in body control."

Slender though it was, it was six shaku of solid iron.

No human's muscles could throw it, and even swinging it was far from easy.

Only by using mana to control the body, then stabilizing the core, could this iron javelin fly where it was aimed.

Most of those using the iron javelins here were new recruits, and the primary goal was to teach them how to handle virtual musculature and their bodies.

Once they learned to move their bodies without relying on physical muscle, the same principles applied to actual javelin throws and sword drills.

"Since we've grown from a hundred-man unit, Krische wanted to train them in things beyond melee combat, so javelin and archery drills are the main focus right now."

"Archers?"

"Yes. The Kuro-Fuyo operates independently a lot, so Krische wants them to be able to coordinate ranged and close-quarters action within the unit. Krische heard from Nyan-nyan that Ojii-sama used to do something similar."

"……Nyan-nyan?"

"Kokys."

Gallen's eyes widened at the word, then after a beat, he laughed with genuine amusement.

"Haha, Kokys is Nyan-nyan, is he? I see――pair it with his helmet and the name becomes quite entertaining."

"Ehehe, right, Ojii-sama!? Krische thinks Nyan-nyan is a really good nickname!"

What an absurd nickname――hearing the name "Nyan-nyan" bestowed upon his former subordinate Kokys, Gallen laughed heartily and stroked Krische's head.

Gallen saw absolutely nothing wrong with the penalty-game-grade naming inflicted on Kokys.

Whatever his beloved granddaughter wanted――strict with both himself and others, Gallen made an exception for Krische alone.

Taking his approval as praise, Krische nuzzled against Gallen, her cheeks softening with delight.

*Ojii-sama really understands*, she thought, in high spirits.

What followed was a negative spiral.

Having seen her grandfather's reaction, Krische grew ever more confident in her naming sense.

With her abysmal naming sense now bolstered by unfounded confidence and the authority of Alberinea, Krische was like a natural disaster descending without warning――her menace grew by the day.

Vulture――Dagra――felt a pang of sympathy for the newest victim, Kokys, and for those yet to come, and glanced at Mia beside him.

*Don't look at me*, Mia seemed to say, shaking her head vigorously.

It was Kalua's fault, not hers.

Being blamed for this was simply too unjust.

"……Indeed, something like that did happen. During a retreat, I gathered stragglers and formed an improvised fire-support unit."

This was from the days when Gallen had led Bogan and the others.

They had lost that battle due to an incompetent general and were under fierce pursuit, left with nothing to do but pray for luck.

Since it had come to that, Gallen resolved to counterattack the enemy's pursuing commander.

He could see that the enemy had grown overconfident in their victory.

Gallen had Bogan and the others round up stragglers――and from them, he assembled a small javelin unit and an archery unit.

If spear and shield created stalemate in battle, then ranged weapons――the one-sided advantage they provided――created superiority, and the smaller the engagement, the more decisively that superiority determined the outcome.

An army-wide advantage of two thousand against tens of thousands changed nothing, but stripping twenty men from the hundred in front of you had an overwhelming impact on the soldiers of both sides.

On a battlefield where tens of thousands of lives clashed, it was a trifle, but no human could perceive anything beyond the advantage or disadvantage created by the few dozen in their immediate vicinity.

He gave spears to those with low morale, assigning them as javelin troops and forbidding them from close combat.

Unskilled throws or not, against pursuing cavalry, the targets were large.

Above all, numbers were what mattered.

He used himself as bait, standing before the enemy and inviting a charge, then ordering a volley at the committed horsemen.

Having created an advantage, he led the counterattack into the confusion and routed the enemy――

"A fortress of shields, yet with decisive striking power――I see, add archers for superiority and there are no gaps at all."

What Krische was building in the Black Flag Special Service was a miniature army.

The Black Flag was already composed entirely of mana users――the finest infantry, who also served as formidable cavalry.

If the ability to create superiority through ranged fire were added on top, no enemy could stand against them.

"The Kuro-Fuyo's weakness is that everyone's swordsmanship is kind of bad, so Krische wanted a way to one-sidedly defeat opponents who are purely stronger in close combat. Lots of them died in the last battle, after all."

"……I am sorry. If I had managed things a little better, the casualties might have been fewer."

"Ojii-sama isn't to blame. Krische heard from Selene that Ojii-sama did an amazing job."

If someone gets in Krische's way, Krische has to kill them. So Krische is asking Lila like this, so that it doesn't come to that."

The blade did not waver.

It was truly no joke, none at all.

The sight of her extraordinary display moments ago told Lila that these words were no idle threat.

She was completely serious about this.

"Krische's goal is not the Kreisharana — it's the dragon's heart. But if Lila refuses to guide Krische, then Krische has no choice but to accept it. ……So please, think carefully before you answer."

# Chapter 145: A Brief Introduction to the Setting, etc.

*(T/N: This is an author's note chapter containing world-building information and a "simulation game"-style overview of the nations. Translated in full below.)*

**※ A brief introduction to the setting, etc.**

**■ —— Setting**

*Note: The information presented here is generally limited to what the people of this world could know at present.*

**○ Mana**

A force that has existed since ancient times — mysterious particles that drift within bodies and through the atmosphere.

They respond to the will of living things, taking a semi-physical form known as a 'spiritual substance.' Normally they drift through the air, but can sometimes gather into a liquid-like state, and in underground deposits they crystallize into what are called magic crystals. Their properties remain poorly understood even now. In the atmosphere they tend to disperse, while in stable media like water and earth they tend to attract one another — yet they sometimes behave contrary to this tendency, continuing to puzzle researchers.

They appear to have the ability to interact with, decompose, and generate matter, but the detailed principles remain unexplained.

Some researchers theorize that this power seeps in from an overlapping parallel world, while others hypothesize that the world itself is formed from mana — but all such views remain mere conjecture based on observed properties.

**○ Mana-Users**

No living thing is entirely devoid of internal mana, but those whose bodies have been physically altered by an exceptional concentration of mana are defined as such. The term primarily refers to humans, but in a broader sense also includes magical beasts — mana-bearing animals.

Mana-users are, as described, physically altered. It is said that their bodies contain internal organs made of mana-based spiritual substance. These organs decompose ingested food and waste products into mana — as a result, mana-users no longer require excretion, among other physical changes from ordinary organisms.

The effects of mana on the body are significant. Aging is generally slowed; their bodies tend to stop maturing in young adulthood, with gradual aging beginning only past thirty. However, individual variation is great, and some are said to have remained unchanged in appearance past fifty.

They are also resistant to disease and tolerant of poisons and alcohol. Mana-users are generally viewed as biologically superior to ordinary individuals.

On the other hand — perhaps as a tradeoff — mana-users tend to suffer higher rates of infertility compared to non-users, and the birthrate among nobles, who are almost universally mana-users, skews low. This tendency weakens when one partner lacks mana, but mana-using nobles often find physiological revulsion toward non-users — who age faster and require excretion — and such marriages are generally disfavored.

Most mana-users are hereditary, but spontaneous cases occur. Given that magical beasts are more common in deep forests where mana concentration is high, some researchers believe ambient mana density plays a role.

**○ Magic Crystals**

Crystalline bodies said to form from mana solidifying underground, physically resembling quartz.

Their physical properties are similar to quartz, but within these crystals, mana can be manipulated as within a living body. By inscribing patterns called 'magical formulae' into them, various forms of physical manipulation become possible through mana.

Light, heat, cooling, water attraction — in some cases, energy can be radiated to burn or destroy objects. Applications are varied, but due to poor mana efficiency, they are rarely used as weapons.

Extensive research was conducted in the past, but military applications proved impractical in terms of efficiency. Currently, researchers known as 'mages' conduct personal research to invent new formulae, while also serving as 'magical engineers' specializing in the production of everyday magic crystal devices. In modern times, the two roles are often conflated.

Research into 'magic' — the technique of manipulating and applying mana outside the body or a magic crystal — has been pursued by many mages from past to present, but no breakthrough has yet been achieved.

**○ Phantom Muscles**

A type of magic available to mana-users.

Also called physical enhancement, this refers to the use of mana for bodily augmentation. By constructing a spiritual-substance body overlaid on one's own physical frame, one can control the body and amplify muscular strength, gaining high physical capabilities. While even unconscious activation occurs among mana-users — making the technique itself not particularly advanced — the deeper one attempts conscious mastery, the greater the concentration and mental stability required, and the difficulty rises sharply.

In some cases, the enhanced physical abilities prove uncontrollable, and the resulting recoil can injure the user — a double-edged sword. Particularly in the extreme conditions of combat, even experienced mana-users find it difficult to wield freely. Skill in this technique is said to define a mana-user's worth.

Battlefield mana-users often build heavily muscled bodies, but this is primarily to absorb recoil and control excessive force. Many also wear armor of weights no ordinary person could bear, for stability — yet some warriors believe these very efforts take them further from the ideal.

**○ Magical Beasts**

Mana-bearing animals.

Most are existing species born with mana; generally larger than their base animals, with a tendency toward blue-tinged coloration.

Without exception, they possess excellent mana control. Combined with their massive frames, the resulting physical capabilities far exceed those of human mana-users. Magical beasts are feared worldwide — or, in some regions, worshipped as local deities.

The reason human mana-users do not grow to similar proportions is unknown, though the prevailing theory suggests that human willpower unconsciously suppresses mana's enlarging effect.

Magical beasts are presumed to be long-lived, but their danger makes verification impossible.

Tigers, wolves, hawks, boars — most commonly, beasts native to mountains and forests produce magical variants, though exceptions exist, and it is believed any species may potentially produce a magical beast.

Many maritime accidents are also attributed to magical beasts, and numerous seafaring nations devote considerable effort to countermeasures.

**○ Dragons**

Also referred to as ancient dragons or holy spirits.

Intelligent beasts said to have once ruled the world. Most notable are their immense mana reserves and colossal bodies. Their fortress-like frames and mineral-like scales, their mana beyond any magical beast's measure — ancient records state that a dragon's roar reshapes the landscape.

In the distant past, a territorial dispute among dragons — the Dragon War — saw countless dragons rampaging, and many civilizations were destroyed in the devastation. The name of the Lusheran Empire, which once flourished only to be annihilated after provoking a dragon's wrath, is well known.

However, as no sighting of a dragon in flight has been recorded for centuries, dragon lore is often regarded as mere fable. While they remain objects of worship in some regions, skepticism about their existence and abilities persists.

Their blood and flesh are said to contain potent power. Many legends tell of humans who consumed it and gained great strength, and the dragon's body is treated as a kind of elixir.

**■ —— Simulation-Game-Style National Overview (as of Chapter 1)**

**○ Alberan Kingdom — Difficulty: ★★★**

A former superpower located in the continent's relative northwest.

In the past, this nation controlled half the continent. No nation on the continent is ignorant of Alberan, which forged the Sacred Convention with the dragon and established it as international law. Though it lost much territory after splitting with Elsren, it has not lost all its former power and remains a strong nation possessing a formidable military, advanced technology, and robust economy.

However, surrounded by strong powers — Elsren to the east, Elderant to the west, Galshan to the south — all on hostile terms, caution is required. Furthermore, in the main scenario *'The Two Cursed Children,'* the current king has fallen ill from an unknown disease, and a covert succession struggle is underway between the princess and the prince. The royal court seethes with unrest, and a misstep can plunge the player into crisis early on.

A strong nation, yet one requiring care on many fronts.

Also note that this nation occasionally produces individuals of extraordinarily high ability — be wary. They are difficult to identify, and in most cases their personalities are deeply flawed, making them a double-edged sword if mishandled.

The nation's talent pool is already excellent, so early elimination of these individuals is one viable strategy. In post-Elsren-split scenarios, the policy *'Elimination of Cursed Children'* allows for their removal at birth, but it is imperfect and carries significant drawbacks — their exceptional abilities may turn against you.

Whether to use them or eliminate them — that choice alone dramatically changes the play experience. A fascinating nation.

★ Recommended unit: **Light Infantry**

As befitting a former superpower, the army's average capability is uniformly high across the board. The vast accumulated knowledge and expertise from conquering half the continent has produced a military structure still worthy of a king's army, with high redundancy.

Alberan's appeal is being above average in everything. If forced to single out a strength, it would be their light infantry. Trained in Alberan's terrain of forests and mountains, the light infantry are exceptionally capable, and combined with the aforementioned redundancy, they can reliably execute highly sophisticated tactical coordination.

However, their defensive capability is no different from other nations' light infantry — handle them with care.

**○ Holy Elsren Empire — Difficulty: ★★**

A nation that split from Alberan; its territory is more than double Alberan's.

This great power was the catalyst for the superpower Alberan's collapse, and it holds much of Alberan's former territory.

With its staggering economy, population, and diverse military — this is arguably the continent's foremost nation.

It has expanded by conquering and subjugating territories, spreading its state religion for control. The problem, however, lies in the friction between the powerful Pontifical Court and the emperor-led parliament. Beneath the surface, conflicts abound, and politics are unstable. The wealth gap is severe, religious corruption and graft are rampant.

Regional lords hold strong authority and the risk of rebellion is ever-present, requiring periodic wars to redirect their frustrations.

Starting power makes it nearly unbeatable, with ample talent. But neglect domestic affairs and you may find yourself in crisis — a precarious nation.

★ Recommended unit: **Nomad Cavalry**

This vast nation fields diverse units, able to combine the strengths of its many subjects and provinces into a single military. That is its overwhelming advantage — and if forced to choose, the nomad cavalry drawn from the empire's numerous pastoral peoples would be the pick.

Said to ride before they walk, their horsemanship is unmatched. On flat terrain, mounted skirmishing alone can defeat most opponents with ease. The cavalry itself is excellent and numerous, and used well, it's an easy game.

Whether to crush the old rival Alberan to the west or expand eastward — either way, proper use of these units makes it straightforward.

**○ Elderant Kingdom — Difficulty: ★★★★**

A tribal confederation whose territory is mostly mountains and forests.

A collection of tribes once ruled by Alberan. Though it calls itself a kingdom, in reality it is closer to a tribal alliance. Its territory rivals Alberan's, with numerous mines, but the lack of plains means limited farmland, frequent food shortages, no major cities beyond the capital, and a modest economy.

The many tribes spread across the land are mutually hostile, with constant inter-tribal warfare. Technologically, it lags behind its strong neighbors.

An outdated nation where old customs persist — but its advantage lies in mobilization numbers. The warrior-society ethos remains strong: every man, even a farmer, takes up the sword and becomes a fighter instantly, requiring no training. Their primitive lifestyle maintains their biological strength, and the madness of charging through arrow fire in light gear is sure to terrify any opponent.

A cultural backwater harboring immense potential — the direction you steer it changes everything. A rewarding nation.

★ Recommended unit: **Light Archers**

Forest-dwelling, these people produce far more hunters than other nations. Soldiers who normally fight as light infantry can switch to archery as needed.

Short bows mean limited range, but their marksmanship is unparalleled — even in dense forest with poor visibility, their arrows fly true.

They can also hold their own in melee against equal numbers, making them overwhelmingly advantaged in forest combat where formations are difficult to maintain.

**○ Galshan Republic — Difficulty: ★**

A multi-ethnic nation holding vast territory south of Alberan. Once an Alberan vassal state, it broke away during the Elsren split when Alberan was weakened.

The southern territory is largely arid wasteland, but periodic highland torrential rains deposit rich soil along rivers, giving it unrivaled wheat production.

The nation's name derives from these powerful rains — *Galshan* means 'strong rain.' It possesses a unique rain-worship religion.

Long leveraging its abundant wheat through land and sea trade agreements across the continent, it is a wealthy trading power. Since transitioning to a republic, internal affairs have stabilized without upheaval.

Whether to first consume Alberan and Elsren or ignore them and expand elsewhere — the options are many, and none are difficult.

★ Recommended unit: **Beast Soldiers**

They have embraced diverse cultures and peoples, and the pinnacle of this is the military use of animals.

Acquiring beasts used in various lands and learning the techniques to handle them, they have domesticated a wide variety of creatures as they would horses.

Beast soldiers are the product of encountering and crushing every culture and absorbing every lesson — animals are inherently stronger than humans, and mastered properly, no one can break through a frontal assault.

Formations, tactics, combat arts — all were devised against human opponents. Beast soldiers instill terror, and a single deployment can throw the enemy into utter chaos.

**○ Arna Empire — Difficulty: ★★★★★**

A maritime nation north of Alberan.

Long allied with Alberan through a deep historical partnership.

Flanked east and west by mountain ranges with the sea to the north, it has developed metalworking and shipbuilding. Despite its small territory, maritime trade gives it formidable economic power, and the high-quality magic crystals from its abundant mines make it the continent's leader in crystal processing. Exports are dominated by these.

However, its small size means a large army is impossible, and with allied Alberan to the south, overland invasion is impractical. Its powerful navy suggests a naval approach, but that too has its challenges.

Deeply devoted to holy spirit worship, the resulting theocratic governance makes aggressive expansion politically difficult; reforming the internal structure would be a prerequisite.

★ Recommended unit: **Crossbowmen / Heavy Crossbowmen**

Having obtained crossbows from the distant east through maritime trade, they disassembled and improved upon them. Their ranged capability relies heavily on crossbowmen.

Compared to bows, crossbows have slower rates of fire, require direct fire, have shorter effective range, and cost more due to their complex mechanism — but requiring no training to use, their mid-range armor-piercing power and accuracy more than compensate.

For pure defense, no better weapon exists. Arna provides crossbow training to all citizens regardless of age or gender, making every citizen a potential crossbowman — perfectly complementing a nation of small population but high wealth and technology.

In field battles, skilled engineers provide larger repeating ballistae and catapults, giving their warfare a distinctive character.

Naturally, these siege weapons are also applicable to offensive operations, and their technological prowess alone holds the potential to dominate the surrounding world.

**○ Kreisharana — Difficulty: ☆☆**

Descendants of once-mighty warriors.

A tribe that formerly ruled much of the kingdom's north, they were defeated in a past war with Alberan and now live quietly in the mountains of Albyagel to the kingdom's northwest.

An extremely strict warrior society preserving ancient customs. Boys undergo severe combat training from a young age. Even a man who normally tills fields and crafts daily goods becomes a warrior capable of overwhelming trained regular soldiers of other nations the moment he takes up a spear.

The proximity of magical beasts and their ascetic, harsh environment has forged their fighting strength — in terms of average individual soldier capability, they would rank first or second on the continent.

The problem, however, is population.

Their military mobilization rate relative to population is abnormally high, yet total fighting strength barely reaches 1,500.

Their griffin cavalry — rulers of the sky — number fewer than 100, making offensive operations against other nations hopeless. If invaded, even their elite would be overwhelmed by sheer numbers.

An old non-aggression pact stemming from the Sacred Convention with Alberan prevents attack, but Kreisharana is currently on a path of slow extinction.

Yet perhaps serving the holy spirit that once saved them, and fading along the way, is the path they chose.

★ Recommended unit: **Griffin Cavalry**

Individual soldier quality is remarkable, but the truly extraordinary asset is the griffin — the fierce beast they train and ride.

Five feet at the shoulder with great wings, an eagle's beak, and a lion's body — this creature soars freely through the sky, and the griffin cavalry riding them add a new axis to the two-dimensional battlefield.

Cavalry flanking maneuvers are always powerful, and counter-measures exist. But against griffin cavalry wheeling through the sky overhead, no army on the ground can do anything — and the warriors astride them are the tribe's finest.

A well-timed strike can deal a fatal blow to even a vast army. The key to victory for this outnumbered nation lies with the griffin cavalry — of that there can be no mistake.
